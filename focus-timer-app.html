How to use a focus timer effectively

  1. Choose one clear task. Decide what you want to finish before starting the timer.
  2. Pick a realistic session. Start with a manageable block of focused work rather than an ambitious marathon.
  3. Reduce interruptions. Put unrelated tabs and notifications away; use Strict Mode when it suits your session.
  4. Take a real break. Step away briefly, then decide whether to begin another session.
  5. Repeat consistently. A sustainable routine is more useful than occasional extreme study sessions.
