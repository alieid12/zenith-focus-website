## SEO operating rules
- One primary search intent per page; avoid creating near-duplicate pages for tiny keyword variations.
- Put the main phrase naturally in the title, H1, opening paragraph, URL, and useful subheadings where it fits.
- Link related pages contextually; use descriptive anchor text.
- Keep English and Arabic pages genuinely localized and pair them with reciprocal hreflang tags.
- Cite primary studies or authoritative sources for scientific statements; explain limitations and avoid claiming that Zenith itself is scientifically proven.
- Add real screenshots, original examples, and first-hand product explanations where available.
- Do not invent reviews, ratings, author credentials, download counts, user outcomes, or scientific claims.
- Use Search Console query/page reports to find impressions with weak CTR, queries ranking near page one, and pages with clicks but poor app-store conversion.
- Update lastmod only when page content materially changes.
- Measure qualified organic clicks, App Store link clicks, timer engagement and guide-to-product click-throughs; rankings alone are not the goal.
