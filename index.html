Frequently asked questions

What is Zenith?

Zenith is an iPhone and iPad focus timer that turns completed sessions into progress in a growing virtual garden.

Is Zenith a Pomodoro timer?

Zenith is a focus timer that can support structured work sessions. The website also includes a free browser-based timer with common Pomodoro intervals.

What does Strict Mode do?

Strict Mode is designed to encourage commitment to a focus session and discourage leaving the timer.

Does Zenith include focus sounds?

Yes. Zenith includes ambient sound options to help create a calmer work environment.

Is Zenith available on Android?

Zenith is currently available for iPhone and iPad through the Apple App Store.
