How to use a focus timer

  1. Write down one specific task you can make progress on.
  2. Choose a realistic interval. 25 minutes is a common starting point, not a scientifically mandatory duration.
  3. Silence non-essential notifications and put distracting apps out of reach.
  4. Work on the chosen task until the timer ends. If your mind wanders, gently return.
  5. Take a short break, then decide whether to repeat or adjust the next interval.

What is the Pomodoro Technique?

The Pomodoro Technique is a time-management approach commonly associated with 25-minute work intervals separated by short breaks. You can adapt the interval to the task, your energy and your schedule. The timer is a structure for getting started—not a guarantee of productivity or a replacement for good study methods.

Focus timer questions

Is this online focus timer free?

Yes. This browser timer is free to use and does not require an account.

Is 25 minutes the best focus duration?

Not for everyone or every task. Try 25 minutes if you need a simple starting point, then adjust based on task difficulty and how well you sustain attention.

Can I use it as a study timer?

Yes. Use it for reading, practice problems, writing, revision or any single task that benefits from a defined work period.
