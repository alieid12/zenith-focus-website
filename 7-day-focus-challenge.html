Seven days. One small promise to yourself.

Start a manageable study routine with one focused task each day. This challenge is free to use, print and share with classmates. You do not need Zenith to take part.

Go to your tracker

Before your first session

Choose a subject, a specific task and a time you can protect. Start with 10–25 minutes, then take a break. Adjust the length to suit you; completing the task matters more than a perfect streak. Use any timer, including our free browser timer.

Day 1: Start small

Choose one task you can begin in 10 minutes. Write exactly what finishing looks like.

Day 2: Clear the desk

Keep only the materials for one task. Silence optional notifications and put distracting devices out of reach.

Day 3: Recall first

Close your notes and write what you remember. Check your notes afterward and choose one gap to practise.

Day 4: Protect one block

Reserve a realistic 25-minute block, or a shorter interval if needed. Take a short break afterward.

Day 5: Return to a hard topic

Revisit one topic from earlier this week. Try a practice question before rereading the solution.

Day 6: Work alongside someone

Invite a friend to study at the same time. Each person chooses their own task; share what you completed afterward.

Day 7: Review and repeat

Record what helped you start. Schedule your next three sessions at times that fit your actual week.

For study groups and university clubs

Share this page in a channel where study resources are welcome. Everyone chooses their own subject and session length. Check in once at the end of the week about what worked. Participation, downloading an app and sharing personal progress are all optional.

Invitation you can copy: “I’m trying one focused study session a day for a week. Want to join? Here’s a free printable challenge: https://alieid12.github.io/zenith-focus-website/7-day-focus-challenge.html — use whatever timer works for you.”
